Co-Housing

Our
model.

Your own private space, with all the comfort and privacy of a home — and, right beside it, a warm community and shared areas to share your day.

Become a Member Ask us

Your private space

A home of your own, not a room.

Each resident has 21 m² entirely their own: a bedroom, their own bathroom, a study and wardrobes. You share a comfortable lounge with a sofa and a kitchen with one other person — someone who fits with you, or a friend of yours.

  • 21 m² of private space per person
  • Your own bathroom, study and wardrobes
  • A shared lounge & kitchen with one other person or a friend
  • For couples: 27 m² with a double bed — the whole apartment (~50 m²) is theirs

Shared spaces

The heart of the community.

Downstairs is the heart of the home: spaces to meet, relax and enjoy yourself, whenever you feel like it.

  • Lounge & library
  • Outdoor area for barbecues & gatherings
  • Garden
  • Private parking
  • Gym / exercise room
