Why Greece

A place to blossom again.

Mediterranean life

Sun, sea, fresh food and a pace of life that does you good — all year round.

A lower cost of living

Everyday life in Greece costs significantly less than in most Western countries.

A ready-made community

You do not arrive in a strange place alone. You step straight into a circle of people who fit with you.
