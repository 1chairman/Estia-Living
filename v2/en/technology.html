What we use

Five pillars of technology.

Smart Home

Lighting, heating and safety automation that makes everyday life simple, comfortable and secure.

Sustainability

Energy upgrades, solar power and heat pumps — lower costs and a smaller footprint.

People Matching

Dedicated software (in development) that helps compatible people live well together — the heart of our model.

Reporting & Info

A central information and complaint-reporting platform — one that other providers, subscribers and apartment buildings could use too.

Location Algorithm

Choosing the most suitable area based on the needs of the people who have expressed interest.
