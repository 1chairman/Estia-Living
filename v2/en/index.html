Our Mission

Loneliness is not inevitable.

The shortage of quality homes in beautiful locations, the difficulty of accessing healthcare, and the loneliness of city life gave us the drive to act.

Our mission is to turn the decades after 60 into the most fulfilling period of life — with autonomy, safety and without the anxiety of isolation.

What's included

Everything you need, in one place.

Utilities & Bills

Full coverage of electricity, heating, water and telephony. No surprises, no worries.

Cleaning

Professional cleaning of your apartment every week — a home that always feels fresh.

Shared Spaces

Lounge, library, garden and a barbecue area — where friendships are born.

Monthly Doctor Visit

The doctor comes to you. Regular check-ups, prescriptions and preventive care.

High-Speed Connectivity

Fibre throughout — so you stay close to the people you love.

24/7 Safety Support

An emergency system with a personal SOS button — because safety is non-negotiable.
